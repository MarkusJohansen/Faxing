## Why

The archive calls itself «en levende tidslinje over hver dyst» and draws a road through the
years. Den andre orden was founded on 10 October 2024, and the run of Faxinger crosses that day
without saying so: nothing in the archive marks where the first order's Faxinger end and the
second's begin. Only `Kongerekka` says it, with a dashed line between the reigns.

A reader who wants to know whether a Faxing belongs to the old order or the new one has to know
the date by heart and compare it with each card.

## What Changes

- **The chronology carries the same dashed mark.** Between the newest Faxing of the first order
  and the one above it, the archive draws the dashed line the crown lines draw, labelled «Den
  andre orden stiftes» with the day, from the one constant `SECOND_ORDER_FOUNDED`.
- **Nothing is drawn when the mark would separate nothing.** A chronicle of the second order
  alone, or of the first order alone, is drawn without it — the rule the crown lines already
  follow.
- **Nothing else about the archive changes.** The list stays newest-first, grouped by year, and
  the mark sits inside the year section where the two orders meet, wherever that happens to be.
