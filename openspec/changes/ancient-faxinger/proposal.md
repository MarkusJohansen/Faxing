## Why

Den andre orden was founded on 10 October 2024, and the archive holds Faxinger from both sides of
that day. Nothing on the cards says which are older: a night from 2022 looks exactly like one from
last month.

A dashed divider drawn through the run of Faxinger was tried and refused: it cut the chronology's
road in two to say something the cards themselves can say. The nights of the first order should
carry the age in their own colour instead — the oldest Faxinger are a different, older thing, and a
reader should see that without reading a date.

## What Changes

- **The cards of den første orden are drawn as ancient.** A Faxing held before the founding is
  drawn in older stone with a tarnished brass bevel and aged text, from the same constant the crown
  lines read.
- **Officiality still reads.** An official ancient night keeps the lighter ground the official
  cards always have; the two readings sit beside each other instead of competing.
- **The legend names it.** The archive's legend gains «Den første orden» beside the two marks it
  already explains.
- **No line is drawn.** The run of Faxinger stays one road through the years; nothing is inserted
  between two cards.
